ABSTRACT 
In illustrative implementations of this invention , a crucible 
kiln heats glass such that the glass becomes or remains 
molten . A nozzle extrudes the molten glass while one or 
more actuators actuate movements of the nozzle , a build 
platform or both . A computer controls these movements 
such that the extruded molten glass is selectively deposited 
to form a 3D glass object . The selective deposition of molten 
glass occurs inside an annealing kiln . The annealing kiln 
anneals the glass after it is extruded . In some cases , the 
actuators actuate the crucible kiln and nozzle to move in 
horizontal x , y directions and actuate the build platform to
