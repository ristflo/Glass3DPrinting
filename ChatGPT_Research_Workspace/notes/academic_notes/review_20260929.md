# Evidence review of chapters 03–07

Reviewed 2026-09-29. Scope: saved chapters, academic/commercial source ledgers and bibliography fragments; targeted primary-source checks of current claims. Chapters and master bibliography were not edited by this reviewer. This is an actionable integration review, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage.

## Fix before final compilation

1. **Undefined citation:** chapter 07 cites `com_chhadeh` twice; neither bibliography fragment defines it. Replace with the existing `acad_chhadeh2026`. All other chapter 03–07 citation keys resolve against the union of saved academic/commercial fragments.
2. **Complete article metadata:** `com_liu` is volume 11, issue 2, **460–466** (2024), DOI `10.1089/3dp.2022.0112`. Confirmed by live Crossref API and [KTH author publications](https://kth.diva-portal.org/smash/person.jsf?pid=authority-person:74530). `acad_baudet2019` should use pages **2307–2317**, issue 5, rather than only 2307; see [publisher record](https://opg.optica.org/spotlight/summary.cfm?id=409127). Crossref gives only the first page for this item.
3. **Separate access year from publication year in JSON:** several undated institutional/vendor sources have `year: 2026` solely because accessed in 2026. Academic BibTeX already omits unknown dates; commercial entries explain the convention in a note. Prefer a null/omitted publication year plus the actual `accessed` date in ledgers, and a consistent undated convention in the final bibliography. In particular, `acad_mcgill` explicitly says its publication year is unverified but still has `year: 2026`.
4. **Minor text correction:** chapter 03, first paragraph: “a inventory” → “an inventory”; simplify “increasing viscosity halves flow if viscosity doubles” to “doubling viscosity halves predicted flow.”

## Verified and useful for integration

- **Chhadeh 2026 is verified, not a prospective reference.** Publisher search record and [Crossmark](https://crossmark.crossref.org/dialog/?doi=10.1038%2Fs44334-026-00072-1) confirm publication on 3 April 2026, *npj Advanced Manufacturing* 3, article 16. The saved publisher HTML verifies the approximately 40% difference between the means of 90° and 0° specimens, and the explicit departure from EN 1288-3 in specimen dimensions and roller freedom. Existing chapter 05 wording preserves those qualifications. Publisher direct browsing redirected to an inaccessible authentication endpoint; the live search record and saved primary full text were used together.
- **Maple operating data can improve the comparison table.** The Chhadeh Methods section reports a Maple 4 nozzle-tip thermocouple reading of **940 °C**, a **500 °C chamber**, and a **1.5 mm outlet**. These are experimental operating conditions, not a rated maximum. Chapter 07 may retain “maximum NR” but add “940 °C in cited experiment.” The 90°/0° labels concern infill orientation in the tested specimen; toolpath length and thermal history are coupled and should not be described as isolated material anisotropy.
- **CORDIS figures match.** [NOBULA grant 190122644](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/190122644) reports EUR 2,499,875 EU contribution, EUR 3,571,250 total project cost, end date 30 April 2026, and now “Project closed.” The saved chapter correctly avoids treating project funding as machine cost or total company fundraising. An optional precision improvement is “completed commercialization project” when referring specifically to that grant.
- **Evenline specification matches.** The [live technology page](https://www.evenline.co/technology) retains Mark III, 325 × 325 × 380 mm, operation at 2000 °F, ceramic nozzle, manual separation and overnight cooling in another chamber. The stated 1093 °C conversion is correct. These remain supplier claims.
- **Nobula current specification gap remains real.** The [live page](https://www.nobula3d.com/) still presents laser fabrication without a numerical machine specification table. No current temperature rating, machine price, or accepted-part yield should be inferred.
- **UpNano process data match.** Its [23 July 2024 announcement](https://www.upnano.com/novel-process-for-3d-printing-macro-sized-fused-silica-parts-with-hi-res-features/) reports 600 °C debinding, 1300 °C sintering and approximately 30% isotropic shrinkage. The chapter correctly labels these as material/process-specific supplier information.
- **Fraunhofer IPT is correctly classified as tooling, not glass deposition.** The [German primary project page](https://www.ipt.fraunhofer.de/de/projekte/3dheat.html) verifies July 2025–June 2027, steel LPBF tool bodies and wire laser deposition for the glass-contact layer. English-page fetch timed out. The project goal of doubling tool lifetime must not be reported as an achieved result.
- **Chen date is correctly qualified.** DOI `10.3390/mi16010029` has online publication 28 December 2024 and a 2025 volume/issue citation. [Publisher record](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-666X/16/1/29) and [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39858685/) confirm PID adjustment of laser power. Keep the issue-year/online-year distinction.

## Remaining limitations to disclose

The academic directory deliberately retains narrower evidence or explicit unknowns for McGill and Nagaoka; no newly verified direct inorganic molten-glass apparatus was established for either in this review. The report should not imply that every institution listed operates a glass printer. No independent current vendor price or production-yield evidence was found. The simplified nozzle, heat-transfer, stress, and cost equations are framed as models with assumptions; this review identified no substantive error requiring replacement. Citation verification does not establish that the literature search is systematic or exhaustive.
